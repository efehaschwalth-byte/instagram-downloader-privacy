# Privacy Policy — Instagram Video Downloader HD

**Last updated:** November 21, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
Instagram HD Downloader does not require user account registration and does not directly collect personal identity data such as your name, password, or Instagram credentials on our servers.

## 2. Media Links & Downloaded Files
Links you paste or enter into the app are processed solely to retrieve and download publicly available media files you request. Downloaded videos and audio files are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to or stored on a developer-owned database.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
This app uses Google AdMob (App Open, Interstitial, and Banner ads) to display advertisements. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers, IP address, coarse location, and ad interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy (`https://policies.google.com/privacy`) to serve and measure ads.

## 4. Clipboard & Storage Access
Clipboard access is used only when you paste a link or when automatic link detection is active to fill the URL field. Storage / media access is used solely to save and open downloaded files on your device.

## 5. Children's Privacy
This app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
